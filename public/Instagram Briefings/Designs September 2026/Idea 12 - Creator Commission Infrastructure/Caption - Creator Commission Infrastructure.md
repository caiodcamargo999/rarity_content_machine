## EN

TikTok Shop paid out close to sixteen billion dollars in creator commissions in the United States last year. That number more than doubled in twelve months, and eMarketer already has it clearing twenty billion this year. None of that money moved as a flat fee for a single video. Every dollar was tied to an actual sale.

Goldman Sachs has the whole creator economy nearly doubling to roughly 480 billion dollars by 2027, and the growth is not sitting in the budget line most DTC brands still call influencer marketing. It is sitting in the infrastructure underneath it: a link, a code, a percentage that keeps paying the creator every time someone else buys. That is a different relationship than paying once for a mention and hoping the algorithm cooperates that week.

A brand that negotiates a flat rate for one post is buying a single afternoon of someone else's attention. A brand that sets up a real commission structure is buying a creator's incentive to keep selling for as long as the product holds up, and that incentive does not expire when the post stops getting served.

Save this before you brief your next creator deal.

Guarda esto antes de armar tu proximo trato con un creador.
