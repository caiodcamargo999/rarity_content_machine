# Caption — Idea 1: AI Shoppers Convert Better Than Humans

Anchor verified live: Adobe Analytics own report (business.adobe.com/blog/ai-traffic-surge-retail-sites-not-machine-readable),
cross confirmed via Digital Commerce 360 — AI referred traffic to US retail sites up 62% YoY in
July 2026, up over 1,200% since October 2024. In March 2026 that traffic converted 42% better than
every other channel, a reversal from converting 38% worse in March 2025.

Opening technique: Thesis / Sharp Claim.

---

## EN

The worst traffic on your analytics dashboard a year ago is the best traffic you are getting right now. Adobe's own numbers confirm it. AI referred visits to US retail sites are up 62% year over year as of July 2026, and in March that same traffic converted 42% better than every other channel combined, a complete reversal from converting 38% worse a year before.

The reason is almost embarrassingly simple once you see it. A search engine sends someone off to browse and compare. An AI assistant only sends the click once it has already decided that specific page is the answer, which is a much higher bar to clear before anyone ever lands there.

Most product pages were never built to clear that bar. They were written for a crawler and a pair of human eyes, not for a model quietly deciding what to recommend, which is exactly why Adobe just shipped a free tool to check whether a page is even legible to one. Most retailers have not run it once.

Screenshot the number. You will need it in your next planning meeting.

La mayoria de los minoristas nunca la ha corrido.

Captura el numero. Lo vas a necesitar en tu proxima reunion de planeacion.
