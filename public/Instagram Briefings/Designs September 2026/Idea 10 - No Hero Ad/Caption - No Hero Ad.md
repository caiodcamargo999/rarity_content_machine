# Caption — Idea 10: No Hero Ad

Opening technique: **Blunt Fact Drop** (Darkroom's playbook is two days old, per the brief's own time
peg — used across all three languages). This supersedes the brief's draft `## CAPTION` field, which
opened on a paraphrase of the on-image headline and carried three hashtags; this skill runs zero

## EN

Darkroom's new Black Friday playbook for TikTok Shop is two days old, and it refuses to name a favorite ad. Instead of locking in one hero creative before the sale opens, it has sellers running four variables against each other at once: the opening hook, the demo style, the offer framing, and the CTA language on screen.

A talking head competes against a pair of hands actually using the product. A bundle competes against a straight percent off and against a limited drop, each with its own CTA. Underneath, discounts step up by the hour, bundles pair a starter kit with an upgrade, and creators drop codes that only work during a LIVE. Three formats carry the whole test: In Feed ads pull in cold audiences, Spark Ads scale whatever is already winning, and LIVE Shopping pulls the crowd into the event.

By the time a single winning ad gets approved, the sellers running this playbook are already days into watching the data decide it for them, and that head start is worth more than any one creative ever will be.

Save this. Send it to whoever on your team actually builds your Black Friday ads this year.
