# Caption — Idea 6: The Skipped Platform

Opening technique: Blunt Fact Drop (TransUnion's 12 channel commerce ROAS ranking, a beat not on the image — the image only carries the 22% CPA line). Supersedes the brief's draft `## CAPTION` field.

## EN

TransUnion ranked twelve ad channels on commerce return, and Meta and Google didn't come out on top. Snapchat did, with the highest indexed ROAS of any of them, and it gets shoppers to buy about half a day faster than the other major social platforms.

Two more data points back it up. In a controlled test Snap ran this July, its AI optimized product ads cut the cost of a new customer by 22% within a week. Over the last Black Friday and Cyber Monday stretch, brands running Northbeam saw acquisition costs on Snapchat drop another 20%, while every other major platform got more expensive.

Most Q4 plans still run on two lines, Meta and Google, because that's what last year's plan said, not because anyone reran the numbers. A channel nobody has retested this year isn't a strategy. It's an assumption with a budget attached.

Screenshot the number. You'll need it in your next planning meeting.

Hay dos datos más que lo confirman. La mayoría de los planes del cuarto trimestre siguen repartidos en dos líneas, Meta y Google, porque así venía en el plan del año pasado, no porque alguien haya vuelto a revisar los números. Un canal que nadie ha vuelto a probar este año no es una estrategia. Es una suposición con presupuesto asignado.
