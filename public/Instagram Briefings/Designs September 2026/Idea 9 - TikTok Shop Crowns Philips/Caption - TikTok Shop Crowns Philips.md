## EN

Zero of the seven brands headlining TikTok Shop's biggest sale of the year were discovered by TikTok Shop. Brand Palooza runs every November, and this year's confirmed lineup is Philips, Benefit Cosmetics, Liquid I.V., HeyDude, Maybelline, Fenty Beauty and The Ordinary, with deals up to 55 percent. Every one of those seven built its name somewhere else first.

TikTok Shop opened in the US in September 2023 on the opposite pitch: an unknown seller could go viral and own the Shop tab by morning. That story still gets told in press interviews. It just isn't what earns the front page once the money gets serious. Over one Black Friday weekend the platform crossed 500 million dollars in US sales, and brands already doing more than 10 million a year grew their sales 76 percent in that same stretch, faster than everyone else on the app.

Discovery got those seven brands famous somewhere else. TikTok Shop's algorithm just decided who deserved the biggest stage once real money was on the table. Whatever platform found your brand first will make the identical call the moment your shelf space is worth fighting over, so build the audience that follows you off the app, not the one the algorithm is currently renting to you.

Save this. Send it to the founder still counting on getting discovered twice.
