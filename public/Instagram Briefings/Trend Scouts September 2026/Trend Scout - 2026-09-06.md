**1. The day nobody was standing between the product and the customer anymore** — Growth Marketing & Branding · Date · Static
Fact: On September 6, 1916 (110 years ago today), Clarence Saunders opened Piggly Wiggly in Memphis, the first true self-service grocery store — customers picked items off the shelf themselves instead of a clerk fetching their order, forcing companies to invent real packaging and brand recognition for the first time.
Angle: Every DTC brand selling through a feed or a checkout page today is solving the exact problem packaging first solved in 1916 — sell yourself with zero human in the room.
Source: https://www.history.com/this-day-in-history/september-6

**2. A DTC fashion brand made $3.5 million in 24 hours by letting customers do the selling** — Growth Marketing & Branding · News · Carousel
Fact: DTC fashion brand Set generated $3.5 million online in 24 hours from its Coastline collection drop by giving regular customers the access usually reserved for influencers, and its $500,000 Austin activation helped acquire thousands of new shoppers.
Angle: The brand didn't hire more influencers, it turned its existing customers into the influencers — the cheapest creator roster a DTC brand already owns is the one it already sold to.
Source: https://www.glossy.co/pop/set-customer-influencer-strategy-coastline-drop/

**3. Fashion's DTC era just got its obituary written** — Business & Strategy · News · Carousel
Fact: With Shein's IPO and Everlane's acquisition both back in the news this week, Glossy reports that surviving DTC fashion brands like Cuyana and Faherty only made it by moving beyond pure-play direct-to-consumer into disciplined inventory, repeat-customer programs, physical stores, and wholesale.
Angle: The brands still calling themselves "DTC-first" are the ones quietly building everything DTC was supposed to make unnecessary — the channel was never the strategy, it was just the cheapest way in.
Source: https://www.glossy.co/fashion/shein-ipo-dtc-fashion-cuyana-faherty-everlane/

**4. A grooming brand found $33,000 hiding on its own tracking page** — Growth Marketing & Branding · News · Static
Fact: DTC brand Dr. Squatch added personalized product recommendations to its order-tracking page and saw a 31.89% click-through rate, generating $32,978 in directly attributed revenue in a single quarter.
Angle: The page every customer already opens to obsessively refresh their shipping status was sitting there unmonetized — the highest-intent screen in the whole funnel was the one nobody was selling on.
Source: https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/09/02/dr-squatch-revenue-post-purchase-tracking-page/

**5. The FTC says Amazon hid a pricing trick from over a million advertisers for seven years** — Business & Strategy · News · Carousel
Fact: The FTC and 22 state attorneys general are suing Amazon, alleging a 2018 change to its ad auction secretly inflated prices for more than a million advertisers over seven years, on a platform that controls roughly three-quarters of the US retail media market.
Angle: If your ad account runs through a single platform you don't control, the price you're paying was never really the number on the dashboard — it was whatever the platform decided you wouldn't notice.
Source: https://www.retaildive.com/news/amazon-hid-surcharges-ad-auctions-alleges-ftc-complaint/829294/

**6. Sephora is turning product launches into a monthly live TV event, on TikTok** — Growth Marketing & Branding · News · Static
Fact: Sephora debuts on TikTok Shop on September 19 with "Sephora Drop Shop," a monthly livestreamed event format featuring celebrity hosts, brand founders, and creator content built around exclusive product drops.
Angle: They didn't just list products on a new channel, they rebuilt the product launch itself as a show people show up for — the format is the acquisition strategy now, not the discount.
Source: https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/09/04/sephora-plans-exclusive-drops-for-tiktok-shop/

**7. AI is already sending 62% more shoppers to online stores than it was a year ago** — Business & Strategy · News · Static
Fact: Adobe Analytics data shows AI-associated referral traffic to e-commerce sites rose 62% year over year in July 2026, and which AI platforms send that traffic keeps shifting month to month.
Angle: The channel most DTC brands haven't built a single asset for is already sending real buyers — optimizing for an AI answer is quietly becoming as important as optimizing for a search result.
Source: https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/09/03/ecommerce-trends-ai-sources-referral-traffic-online-retailers/

**8. A beauty executive used her old industry's playbook to break into a category that hadn't changed in decades** — Mindset & High Performance · News · Carousel
Fact: Lorne Lucree, a beauty industry veteran, launched Wizard Wellness in January with a beauty-brand approach to allergy care; nine months later the line is in Walmart, CVS, and Target.
Angle: She didn't out-formulate the incumbents, she brought a completely different industry's standards for packaging, story, and shelf presence into a category that had never been asked to meet them.

**9. Instagram now weighs a DM three to five times heavier than a like** — Growth Marketing & Branding · IG Trend · Static

**10. A truck driver's lip-sync video is quietly outperforming every polished product ad this week** — Mindset & High Performance · IG Trend · Carousel
