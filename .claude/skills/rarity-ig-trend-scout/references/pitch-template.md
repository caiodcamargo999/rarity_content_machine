## 1. The pitch card format

```
**N. <Short, punchy title, not a full headline>** — <Pillar> · <Anchor type> · <Format guess>
Fact: <the real, specific fact or trigger, one to two sentences, with the concrete number/decision/name>
Angle: <one line — the rough lesson or mechanism this could decode into>
Source: <URL>
```

- **Pillar**: Growth Marketing & Branding | Business & Strategy | Mindset & High Performance
- **Anchor type**: News | IG Trend | Date
- **Format guess**: Static | Carousel — a fast read of the room, not a commitment; `rarity-ig-idea-engine`
  makes the real call once it goes deep.

Present all 10 together as a single numbered list in chat, in this format, so Caio can scan the whole
set in one pass.

## 2. Worked example: 10 real pitches from 2026-07-11

**Note (2026-09-06):** this batch predates Rarity's narrowing to e-commerce/DTC as the content focus
(see `sourcing-playbook.md` section 0). Read it for the pitch-card format, the pillar/anchor-type spread
discipline, and the level of specificity each fact hits — not for subject matter. Several of these
subjects (a QSR sponsorship, a general ad-agency story) wouldn't clear the DTC scoping test today. A
current, DTC-scoped batch lives in `public/Instagram Briefings/Trend Scouts <Month>/`.

**1. Zero-click search is quietly rewriting content strategy** — Growth Marketing & Branding · News · Static
Fact: roughly 68% of US Google searches now end without a click to any website, changing how brands need
to think about visibility.
Angle: you are not losing traffic, you are losing the click, and treating those as the same problem is
why the content strategy keeps missing.
Source: https://agilebrandguide.com/yesterdays-marketing-technology-ai-news-july-8-2026/

**2. 2,000+ brands are already running ads inside ChatGPT** — Business & Strategy · News · Static
Fact: Criteo reports more than 2,000 brands running ads on ChatGPT as OpenAI expands its ad pilot into
the UK, Mexico, Brazil, Japan, and South Korea and tests multi-advertiser ad units.
Angle: the next ad platform did not launch with a pitch deck, it launched with a waitlist nobody had to
be convinced to join.
Source: https://agilebrandguide.com/yesterdays-marketing-technology-ai-news-july-8-2026/

**3. A fintech company stopped looking like a bank on purpose** — Growth Marketing & Branding · News · Carousel
Fact: Elev8 dropped standard fintech iconography for an emotionally led creative direction that reads
closer to a record label than a financial product, part of a broader 2026 "vibe branding" shift.
Angle: the rebrand was not the point, the defection from the category's whole visual language was.
Source: https://www.the-off-label.com/marketing-trends-july-2026/

**4. The most confident brands of 2026 hid their own logo** — Business & Strategy · News · Carousel
Fact: a mid-2026 trend review names "debranding," stripped-back identities that trust the audience to
fill in who the brand is, as one of the defining moves of the year's best campaigns.
Angle: removing your name from the ad is not humility, it is proof you think the audience already knows
you without it.
Source: https://www.famouscampaigns.com/2026/06/the-12-trends-that-defined-brand-marketing-in-2026-so-far/

**5. A supermarket turned a lettuce into a World Cup mascot** — Growth Marketing & Branding · News · Static
Fact: Tesco gave iceberg lettuces a football makeover to ride one of the year's biggest sporting moments,
without buying an official sponsorship.
Angle: you do not need the rights to the tournament, you need the nerve to show up next to it first.
Source: https://famouscampaigns.substack.com/p/the-best-campaigns-this-week-149

**6. A charity rebuilt a 1990s video shop instead of running an ad** — Mindset & High Performance · News · Carousel
Fact: Smartphone Free Childhood built a working recreation of a 1990s video rental shop in Slough to make
its point about kids and phones, rather than buying media space to say it.
Angle: they did not argue the point, they built a place where the argument makes itself the moment you
walk in.
Source: https://famouscampaigns.substack.com/p/the-best-campaigns-this-week-149

**7. The trend built entirely on other people's music taste** — Growth Marketing & Branding · IG Trend · Carousel
Fact: "Headphone Swap," two people trading headphones and reacting to each other's completely different
music, is one of the strongest-performing Reels formats this month.
Angle: the format has no product in it at all, and that is exactly the mechanism worth stealing: borrowed
curiosity outperforms a pitch every time.
Source: https://napoleoncat.com/blog/instagram-reels-trends/

**8. Reels shares are up 67% year over year, and it is not because the videos got better** — Business & Strategy · IG Trend · Static
Fact: Instagram Reels shares rose 67.19% year over year with engagement up 24.76%, according to current
trend tracking.
Angle: everyone optimizes for watch time when the number that actually compounds reach is whether a
stranger handed your video to a friend.
Source: https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/instagram-reels-trends

**9. The only brand whose name is also its own marketing holiday** — Growth Marketing & Branding · Date · Static
Fact: July 11, 7-Eleven's own numeric name, is "Free Slurpee Day," a promotion the company has run for
decades built entirely on the date matching the brand.
Angle: they did not buy a holiday, they noticed their name already was one, and nobody else can ever
compete for July 11.
Source: https://on-this-day.com/onthisday/thedays/alldays/jul11.htm

**10. The aesthetic vlog is losing to the messy, urgent take** — Mindset & High Performance · IG Trend · Static
Fact: current Instagram trend tracking shows timely discourse and utility content outperforming slow,
polished aesthetic vlogs this month.
Angle: polish was never the thing people saved and shared, urgency was, and chasing the first one instead
of the second is a mindset problem dressed up as a content problem.
Source: https://lightreel.ai/blogs/whats-trending-on-instagram

**Pillar spread in this batch:** 5 Growth Marketing & Branding, 3 Business & Strategy, 2 Mindset & High
Performance. **Anchor spread:** 6 News, 3 IG Trend, 1 Date. Real search results skew toward whatever the
news cycle is dense with that week (AI and platform news, this time), so a batch will rarely split
perfectly evenly. What matters is that the search actively reached for the underrepresented pillars and
anchor types rather than just taking the top 10 hits in the order they came back.

## 3. The handoff format once Caio picks one

Once Caio names a number or subject, close with a short, direct summary, for example:

> Going with #9: 7-Eleven's Free Slurpee Day. Real fact: July 11 promotion built on the brand's own name
> matching the date, running for decades. Suggested pillar: Growth Marketing & Branding. Source:
> on-this-day.com. Handing this to `rarity-ig-idea-engine` now to build the full brief.

Then proceed into `rarity-ig-idea-engine`'s own workflow with that subject already established, so its
trigger-finding step is skipped and it moves straight to verifying and deepening the fact this skill
already found.
