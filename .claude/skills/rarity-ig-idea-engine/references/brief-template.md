Output every idea in exactly this structure. It is the handoff contract to
`rarity-ig-designer`. Keep the **Headline**, **Images** and **Visual direction** fields clean and
literal — the design step reads them directly. Fill English, Spanish, AND Portuguese (Brazilian)
everywhere copy appears — never just EN + ES.

Copy everything below the line into the saved brief and fill it in. Delete the carousel block if the
post is static; delete unused optional lines.
